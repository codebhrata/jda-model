Bye-law and approval checklist

Confirm with the sanctioning authority: permissible FAR for your abutting road width, ground coverage, front/side/rear setbacks, maximum height, parking (ECS) norms, and any premium or additional FAR options.

Buildings above roughly 15 m generally need Fire NOC; lifts, stair width, rain water harvesting, and sewage/solid waste provisions follow the byelaws. Projects above 500 sq m land or more than 8 apartments need Bihar RERA registration, with 70% of collections held in a designated account for construction and land cost. Sale price here is treated as excluding GST; GST on residential sales is not added to revenue.
